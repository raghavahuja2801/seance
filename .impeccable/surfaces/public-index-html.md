# Séance: surface brief

Scope: the whole web experience (`public/`): the bill (loader, intro and spirit picker), the stage (the live séance) and the notices (endings). Visitor mode: **Experience**. The work leads from the first viewport.

Audience: the public, arriving from a Halloween share, often on a phone. Job: be talking to a spirit within moments, and leave believing the voice acted, the agent was alive, and it worked the board. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (board and planchette, six spirits and copy, Fish Audio × LiveKit lockup stay; no background audio; no build step).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The séance is a night at an 1890s stage-spiritualism show. You read the bill, choose an act, and the spirit's lines are set in giant woodtype as they're performed. It refuses the category default: a purple candlelit parlour, a centred title over a grid of equal cards, and chat captions.

OWN-WORLD: Two-colour letterpress on midnight-indigo stock. Bone ink for type and rules, oxblood as the second impression, and gaslight gold only as light (footlights, glow), never as ink. Mixed woodtype stacks (fat-face slab, condensed gothic, period roman), playbill rules, fleurons and stars. Ink texture and a slight red/bone misregistration that settles on print. Controls are printed billing lines and stamped buttons.

STORY: You see a bill announcing "An Evening with the Departed", hear each act's own voice, pick one, and raise the curtain. Velvet curtains part on the act. On stage the spirit's words fill the frame; a real, worn Ouija board lies on the table below, recording what it spells; candles around it die as the truth nears. The curtains fall, and a printed notice closes the act.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-height playbill. A small lockup on top, then "★ For One Night Only ★" in condensed caps, then SÉANCE in fat-face across the full measure, then "An Evening with the Departed" and one ritual line ("Speak aloud. The departed will answer."). Below that the six acts are billed in mixed type: name, epithet, ▶ hear, and SUMMON on each row. The primary action is SUMMON on each act.

FORM: Stage-spiritualism playbill, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:90e28dea3f6d4283. Signature interaction: the two-pass print. Every reveal (the bill in the loader, each spirit caption word by word, each notice) inks oxblood then bone, settling out of misregistration. Raises:
- From the arcade marquee: each act previews its real Drama 3 opening line before you commit.
- From ebru: spelled letters stay inked on the board.
- From the coiled tower: revealed words build an ascending record under the board.
- From the tensegrity column: listening, thinking and speaking are structurally distinct (stamp, printer's ornament, live type).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance


## Refinement (2026-10-20, user feedback)
"The ouija board looks off, make it more realistic, along with the candle; what happened to the curtains?"
- The board returns to a realistic object: aged wood with grain, worn edges and varnish; period serif lettering; a wooden planchette with a glass lens; seen in perspective on the table.
- Realistic wax candles around the board replace the footlights. Same "goes out as the truth nears" mechanic.
- A proscenium with oxblood velvet curtains and a fringed valance frames the stage. It parts on summon and falls before the notice. This is the stage promise the direction made and the first build skipped.
